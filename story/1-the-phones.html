Chapter 1·Jan 2020 – Apr 2021Sykes

Learning on the phones

In short: my first job was customer support for a US bank. A few months in, I became the person the floor came to with the hard calls.

My career started in a call center at Sykes, the company now called Foundever. I joined in January 2020, so my first year of work was also the first year of the pandemic.

The account was a US bank. I started at level one: statements, checking and savings accounts, retirement accounts, one American customer after another. It was phone after phone, and never the same customer twice. My English improved a lot there, on real calls with real problems.

The person in the side room

About four months in, I moved into a subject-matter expert role on the floor. I stopped taking my own calls. Instead, when an agent had a call they weren't sure about, often someone new, they took the customer into a side room and I listened in. Then I told them what to do.

Sometimes the problem was language: the agent couldn't quite follow what the customer was saying, so I clarified what the customer meant. After the calls, I gave feedback to the agents and to our team lead.

Where escalations come from

While my manager was on leave, I reviewed the team and noticed a pattern: some agents were escalating calls because of gaps in what they knew. I reported it to my manager, who acted on it.

In 2021, Amadeus offered me a technical support role, and I took it.

What I took with meWorking English with US customers, coaching people in the middle of a live call, and an eye for where escalations start.
