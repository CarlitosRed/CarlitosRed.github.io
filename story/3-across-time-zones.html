Chapter 3·Aug 2023 – Jan 2024VMware

Working across time zones

In short: a contract business analyst role on VMware's partner programs team, where I learned to prove my work from far away.

VMware hired me as a business analyst on contract, on the partner programs team. Most of my day was in Salesforce, with Power BI reports and Excel next to it: exporting data, building formulas and macros, and shaping the numbers into something people could use.

I coordinated Data, BI and Analytics workstreams: requirements sessions, priorities, status reports and following up on deliverables. I cleaned data in Excel for mass imports into Salesforce with Data Loader, checking its quality first. For partner proposals, I checked contracts, found the data in internal systems, granted new licenses to partners and entered it all in Salesforce.

Proving it from far away

I was new, and I was working while travelling in Mexico, in a different time zone from most of the people I needed answers from. Waiting a day for a reply could put my deadlines at risk. So I learned to manage myself: plan around the gaps, work my cases alone, and work directly with senior stakeholders inside the company.

Within about five months I moved up from junior to associate level. My manager's praise was simple: I was willing, and I picked up new tasks easily.

Freedom, trust and the chance to propose my own ideas: that was what I enjoyed most there.

Then Broadcom acquired VMware, and the company I had joined was changing. A Snowflake recruiter reached out, and I applied.

Tools in this chapterSalesforce, Salesforce Data Loader, Power BI (basic), Excel (formulas, macros).
