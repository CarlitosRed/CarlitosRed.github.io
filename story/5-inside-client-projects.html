Chapter 5·Aug 2025 – May 2026Varicent

Inside client projects

In short: as a Business Consultant I worked inside client implementations, turning how a client really runs their numbers into tested configuration.

I joined Varicent, a Canadian software company, through a referral. My job was to sit inside client projects, in teams of five or six, and fit the product to how each client actually worked.

Check that you understood, before you build

Mondays started with the client, then with our own team. I presented what I had done, took the client's requests back, worked out a solution and delivered it. Once, to be sure I had understood how a client ran their payroll numbers, I built a small demo of it in the tool and checked it with them first.

The building happened in Varicent Designer: imports, tables, calculation rules and workflow components, each one tested. When numbers didn't match, I investigated with SQL and Excel, found the root cause and reconciled the results, then validated the outputs in QA and UAT. I documented the expected results, the test evidence and the steps, so the work could be repeated.

Building a small demo first was how I checked I had understood. Still, I preferred owning one process end to end, as I had at Snowflake.
Tools in this chapterVaricent Designer, SQL, Excel, QA/UAT, requirements and client meetings.
